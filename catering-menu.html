Livite was founded by Laurie and Steve with one simple goal: to create a place where food feels good — for your body, your lifestyle, and your community.

Laurie, a registered dietitian, brings a deep understanding of balanced, wholesome eating. Steve, with over 20 years in the restaurant industry, brings a passion for hospitality, thoughtful menus, and food done right.

Every menu is crafted with balance in mind — fresh, flavorful, and designed to fit real life. When you order Livite catering, you're getting the same care we put into every plate at our counter, scaled for your team.

CATERING FAQ

HOW FAR IN ADVANCE SHOULD I PLACE MY ORDER?
24–48 hours recommended. Last-minute? Email us and we'll do our best.
HOW DO I PLACE AN ORDER?
· Email catering@livite.com
· Order online at livite.com
IS THERE A MINIMUM?
$100 before tax and delivery.
WHAT IS THE DELIVERY FEE?
$30 flat delivery fee within 25 miles of Brookline. Covers professional delivery and careful handling. Not a driver gratuity.
DO YOU DELIVER TO MY AREA?
We deliver within 25 miles of our Brookline location. That covers most of Greater Boston, the South Shore, MetroWest, and the North Shore.
WHAT IS THE DELIVERY WINDOW?
30-minute scheduled window. Please have someone available to receive.
DO YOU OFFER PICKUP?
Yes. Same $100 minimum, no delivery fee.
WHAT IS YOUR CANCELLATION POLICY?
24 hours' notice required. Cancellations within 24 hours may incur a fee if food has been prepared.
DOES CATERING INCLUDE PLATES AND UTENSILS?
Yes. Disposable plates, napkins, and serving utensils included with all orders.
CAN YOU ACCOMMODATE DIETARY RESTRICTIONS?
Yes — vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options throughout. Note any allergies when ordering.
NEED HELP PLANNING YOUR ORDER?
Email catering@livite.com and we're happy to help with menu selections and quantities.
